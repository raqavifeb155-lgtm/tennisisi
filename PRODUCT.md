# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Players in Telegram on a phone held upright in one hand, thumb only. They come in for 10–15 minutes, one match, on the metro or at home in the evening, often in a dim room. They spend 30–90 seconds in the menus between matches and 1–5 minutes in the Club between runs. Weak Android phones on the Low graphics preset are common, not an edge case. The secondary audience is the owner's friends testing early builds, who judge everything by whether they find the way in and out without help.

## Product Purpose

TENNISISI is a mobile 3D tennis game with real ball physics and a roguelike on top. A run is an elimination tournament. Skills grow from the strokes you play, and gear drops from opponents and changes the rules of a rally. Success means the player understands every screen within a second, gets from the end of a match to the next match in four taps or fewer, and wants "one more run" because the loot and the build feel worth chasing.

## Positioning

Real tennis that a lucky run turns into super-tennis: the physics stays honest, and the gear you find rewrites it.

## Brand Personality

Sporty, punchy, collectible. The voice is short and direct, in Russian, with numbers in real units (km/h, rpm). The menus should feel like handling physical cards in Balatro: they tilt, shine and flip, and rarity is the one thing allowed to glow. Under the cards, the menus live on top of the 3D court, the way TV tennis graphics sit over the broadcast.

## Anti-references

Generic mobile-game menus made of stacked full-width buttons on a darkened backdrop (what the game has now). Glass-blur panels as the base material. Gradient text. Colored side stripes on cards. Identical icon-plus-heading card grids. Small all-caps labels above every block. Saturated color anywhere except rarities and win/lose states.

## Design Principles

1. The way forward and the way back are always in reach of the thumb. No screen hides its exit below the fold.
2. Rarity is the only thing that glows. One brand color everywhere else, so a purple or red edge means loot at a glance.
3. Cards are objects. Items and rewards are handled, flipped and compared like physical cards, not read like settings rows.
4. Fast by default. Every between-match screen clears in 2–3 taps, and every long animation is skippable with a tap.
5. Show the real number. Stats and level-ups are shown in km/h, rpm and ms, never in abstract percentages alone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Touch targets at least 48 pt, primary actions in the bottom third. Body text at least 4.5:1 contrast on the dark base. Rarity is never shown by color alone: each rarity also has its own shine pattern and label. Long animations are skippable with a tap, and effects scale down on the Low graphics preset.
